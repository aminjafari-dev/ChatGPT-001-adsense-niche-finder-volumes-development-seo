L184: DIY Window Screen Replacement Kits for Easy Assembly and Installation
L185: Window screen replacement kits are a practical alternative to our assembled, custom window screen solutions. Whether you’re a confident DIY homeowner or a novice in the field, our easy-to-follow video guide makes this an approachable DIY project for anyone with the tools and the interest. RiteScreen’s window screen replacement kits are designed for easy assembly and are equipped with two leaf springs and two pull tabs for an even easier installation process.
L186: The replacement window screen kit makes for an easy DIY project that you can be proud of, whether this is your first or tenth window screen replacement. The streamlined assembly and installation process will have you enjoying fresh breezes in as little as 30 minutes!
L187: Take full control over your new window screen’s assembly and reduce risks of damage between the RiteScreen screen shop and your home! Along with mesh and no-mesh options, window screen kits are currently available in 3 widths (36 in., 48 in., and 60 in.) and 2 colors (White and Bronze).

L203: ## Frequently Asked Questions
L204: 
L205: [Select]
L206: 
L207:   * #### Is the size adjustable?
L208: 
L209: Yes, and instructions are included to guide you as to how to cut the frame pieces to adjust down. The largest screen you can make with this kit is a 48" by 48" screen. Thank you for considering RiteScreen!
L210: 
L211:   * #### Can I cut the frame to fit my window?
L212: Yes, you absolutely can cut the frame pieces to fit your specific measurements. Instructions are included for just that. If you have any other questions, please contact us at hello@ritescreen.com. Thank you for your interest in RiteScreen!
L213: 
L214:   * #### How many screens are included in the kit?
L215: 
L216: Each kit contains components for one screen. To build multiple window screens, you will need to buy additional kits or components.
L217: 
L218:   * #### Is spline included in this kit?
L219: Spline is not only included – it is pre-installed into the frame pieces! The DIY window kits are specifically designed as an easy assembly and installation project. Spline is pre-installed on both the mesh and no-mesh window kit options.
L220: 
L221:   * #### Does this window kit include the mesh that goes into the window frame?
L222: Some of our kits include the mesh material used in most of our window screen products while others are frame-only kits with no mesh included. If you already have mesh on hand, the no-mesh window kit options have everything else you need for your DIY window screen replacement project. Please be sure to read the product description for a list of what is included in the kit.
