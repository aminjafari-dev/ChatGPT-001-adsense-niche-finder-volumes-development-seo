Why Sow Motion

## Gardening doesn't have to be complicated.
It just has to be yours.

We believe everyone has a garden in them — a balcony, a backyard, a few pots on a windowsill. Sow Motion exists to give you the tools, knowledge, and encouragement to grow at your own pace, in your own way.

🌱

### Grow your own food

From seed packets to your plate — we help you build the knowledge and habits to grow real food, right at home.

📓

### Track your progress

The Plant Diary gives every plant its own story. Write it down, watch the patterns emerge, and grow smarter every season.

🤝

### Grow together

Gardening is better shared. Join a community of growers who are figuring it out one seed at a time — just like you.

🌿

### Slow down

In a world that moves fast, the garden asks you to slow down. That's not a bug — it's the whole point.
