Build your layers from the bottom up

A terrarium is built in layers, each with a job to do. Start with an inch or two of pebbles or gravel at the base for drainage, so roots never sit in water. Add a thin scattering of activated charcoal next, which keeps the whole thing fresh and helps stave off mold. 
[Image 1: Glass terrarium filled with potting mix]
Some people lay a fine mesh or a layer of moss over that to stop soil sinking into the stones, though it's optional. 
Finally, spoon in your potting mix, a good few inches deep, enough for roots to settle. Reach for cactus compost on open builds and a peat-free houseplant mix for closed ones.

Plant, arrange and style

Now the fun part. Make small wells in the soil and lower each plant in, firming it gently - a long spoon, a pencil or a pair of kitchen tongs works beautifully if your container is narrow. 
[Image 2: Succulent placed in terrarium]
Arrange taller plants towards the back or center and trailing ones near the edge, leaving a little breathing room between them.
