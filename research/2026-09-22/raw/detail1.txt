L80: 
L81: Spline is a type of vinyl cording that fixes the screening material into the frame. Spline is rolled into the grooves using a cite65†spline roller or cite66†screen mouse†www.youtube.com -- tools that are specifically designed to make it easier to press the spline into the grooves in a smooth, fluid motion.
L82: Spline is measured by diameter, which determines the thickness of spline you’ll need to secure your screen to the frame. The charts below can show you which type of spline you should use for your screen. The spline itself is flexible and able to be rolled into grooves that are thinner than it is wide. This is to be sure the spline doesn’t pop out -- and your screen along with it!
L83: ## Selecting Screening & Spline to Use with Common Frames: What Size Screen Spline Do I Need?
L84:   * Measure the spline groove on the screen frame.
L85:   * The spline size should match the spline groove width when using standard fiberglass or aluminum screening or when repairing a damaged screen with the same material. Refer to the chart below for product exceptions.
L86:   * PetScreen^{®}, TuffScreen^{®}, SunTex^{®}, and Super Solar Screening require one size smaller spline due to the thickness of the fabric.
L87:   * Spline sizes are shown in decimals to match fractional spline groove measurements in inches.
