# AdSense niche research — 22 September 2026

## Decision

**Test a site about DIY window and patio insect-screen repair first.** Start narrowly with spline, mesh tension, frame fit and replacement-part identification. Avoid broad “window repair,” “best windows,” and local “near me” keywords.

This is the strongest **provisional** opportunity in this research pass. It is not yet proven to combine high US search volume with low Google competition. Google blocked direct search inspection, so Google rankings and AI Overviews remain unverified; paid keyword-volume and backlink data were not available in this run. A large content investment is not justified by these results alone.

The alternatives are **self-watering container troubleshooting** and **beginner hand-bookbinding troubleshooting**. None has cleared every acceptance gate in the supplied skill.

## What was checked

- Generated **46 candidate ideas**, using community questions, encyclopedia categories, retailer catalogues, manufacturer/support material and model-generated adjacent ideas. These are five idea-source types, not five independent demand measurements.
- Investigated **12 candidates** with autocomplete and search evidence. Nine alternatives received three seed-query research searches each, not full manual ranking audits.
- Manually inspected **10 usable Bing search-result pages for each of the three leading candidates** (30 total). A separate screen pull-tab query failed to expose results and was excluded.
- Collected Google autocomplete responses and worldwide English Wikipedia pageviews; examined a five-year US Google Trends comparison.
- Read manufacturer/maker source pages and current Google AdSense eligibility, RPM and AI-search documentation.
- Preserved exact queries, source links, methods and findings. [Manual search audit](SERP-AUDIT.md), [Trends evidence](TRENDS.md), [candidate inventory](CANDIDATES.md).

**Numbers and evidence labels:** autocomplete counts and displayed pageview/index values are measured observations of their sources. They are **proxies for search demand**. Ranking scores are **proxy judgments**. Revenue figures are **conditional arithmetic scenarios**, not earnings forecasts. No keyword volume, KD, CPC, DR or actual niche RPM has been invented.

Bing was not forced to a US region. Its results help identify competitors and topic gaps but cannot establish a US Google top ten. The Google Suggest requests used hl=en/gl=us, yet some returned international phrases. Unknown-looking websites have not been verified as low-authority domains.

## 1. DIY window and patio insect-screen repair

Homeowners repairing removable insect screens: mesh tension, spline, frame fit, corner keys and retaining hardware. Launch with window-screen troubleshooting; expand to removable patio screen doors after establishing the core.

**Why it leads.** Best fit for an AdSense-first pilot because it combines a durable US maintenance problem with purchasable materials, repeatable inexpensive demonstrations, and multiple specific diagnostic searches. Avoid making the site about all window repair. Original close-up photos, cross-sections, fit measurements, material comparisons and diagnostic flowcharts can give readers a reason to visit.

**Competition evidence, Bing, September 22, 2026.** “Window screen frame bowed after rescreening” included Home Improvement Stack Exchange alongside repair sites. “Remove brittle window screen spline” included Reddit, specialist suppliers and videos. “Window screen spline keeps popping out” produced several pages about the entire screen popping out rather than the spline itself. These are potential intent gaps, not proof that a DR-0 site will rank.

Conversely, “window screen corner replacement” was dominated by products/suppliers; “window screen spline size chart” already had supplier charts and a generated answer. [Phifer’s sizing resource](https://www.phifer.com/screening/diy/sizing/) and [RiteScreen’s rescreening guide](https://www.ritescreen.com/blog/post/how-to-rescreen-window-screens.html) are substantive competitors. A rewritten generic guide would offer little advantage.

**Demand and money.** Google Suggest returned 10 phrases, of which 2 were unrelated screenshot phrases. The US Trends term 'window screen repair' averaged 37 on the shared five-year index, versus 22 for 'self watering planter'. This includes local-service intent and is not entirely available to a content site. Worldwide Window_screen Wikipedia views had a median of 1,044/month, range 725–1,503, September 2025–July 2026. These are demand proxies, not a cluster-volume estimate. Home-improvement Tier B is only an unverified category hypothesis from the supplied skill. RiteScreen lists a DIY frame kit starting at $27.49 (observed September 22, 2026); product purchase intent exists, but this price does not determine publisher RPM. Bing displayed shopping/eBay ads on some sampled queries. No Google ad-density, CPC or publisher RPM measurement was obtained. [Kit source](https://www.ritescreen.com/window-frame-kit.html).

**AI exposure.** Full Bing answers were visible on at least 4 of the 10 sampled queries: spline chart, corner replacement, mesh comparison and plunger-pin replacement. Google AIO exposure remains unknown. Prefer specific diagnosis and original evidence over definitions and generic size tables.

**Scope and risks.** Seasonal demand; local-service searches do not equal article traffic; Phifer, RiteScreen and retail suppliers have strong guides; some general questions receive full generated answers. A tiny site about spline alone is too narrow. Work only on removable insect screens at bench/ground level; insect screens are not fall protection. Access to common US screen profiles is a practical requirement for credible testing.

**Expansion:** removable patio insect-screen doors, mesh durability comparisons, measurement tools and a visual hardware library. Affiliate possibilities include kits, mesh, rollers and replacement hardware; program availability was not verified.

## 2. Self-watering container setup and troubleshooting

Indoor and balcony gardeners choosing, assembling and troubleshooting passive reservoir containers. Focus on wick performance, dry soil despite a full reservoir, overflow, leaks and maintenance.

**Why it remains in contention.** A good low-cost experimental niche: one setup can yield original water-use logs, comparison photos and failure diagnosis. Better expansion potential than an isolated product model; weaker confidence in the commercial and traffic ceiling than screens.

**Competition evidence, Bing, September 22, 2026.** “Self watering planter wick not working” included Gardening Stack Exchange and specialist sites. “Self watering planter reservoir smells” included a Houzz discussion and broad problem guides. The dry-soil query returned some pages about overly wet soil, suggesting imperfect intent matching. However, soil-mix and wick-material searches had multiple close-match guides plus generated answers. A mismatch could also reflect tiny query demand.

**Demand and money.** Google Suggest returned 10 phrases. US Trends five-year average 22 on the shared comparison; complete-year averages remained around 20–22 during 2022–2025, with spring peaks. Sub-irrigated_planter Wikipedia median 189/month, range 135–411, September 2025–July 2026 worldwide. The uncommon encyclopedia label underrepresents broader consumer wording. Current Reddit questions and lifetime video views supply additional interest signals, not monthly search counts. Gardening-equipment Tier B / hobby Tier C is an unverified category hypothesis. Reservoir products and manufacturer support exist; a Bing product-ad block appeared on the algae query. Google advertiser density and actual AdSense RPM were not measured. [Recent community question](https://www.reddit.com/r/vegetablegardening/comments/1t2mco8/self_watering_planter_help/) and [manufacturer support](https://earthbox.gorgias.help/en-US/articles/earthbox-product-info-103478) show actual setup questions and product complexity.

**Risks and expansion.** Strong seasonality outdoors, many closely matched articles, brand-oriented demand, and generated answers on basic soil/material/overflow questions. ## AdSense economics: scenarios, not promises

Google defines [page RPM](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/112030?hl=en) as estimated earnings divided by pageviews, multiplied by 1,000. Therefore revenue = pageviews × page RPM ÷ 1,000.

The following ranges are analyst-selected stress tests, not measured or estimated niche RPMs. The supplied skill’s category tiers were not supported by a current matched publisher dataset, so they are not treated as forecasts.

- **Screens, assumed $5–$15 RPM:** 10,000 monthly pageviews → $50–$150; 30,000 → $150–$450; 100,000 → $500–$1,500.
- **Planters, assumed $4–$12 RPM:** 10,000 → $40–$120; 30,000 → $120–$360; 100,000 → $400–$1,200.
- **Bookbinding, assumed $3–$10 RPM:** 10,000 → $30–$100; 30,000 → $90–$300; 100,000 → $300–$1,000.

All are monthly gross ad revenue before costs/tax. Actual results can be below these ranges. No traffic level or timeline is predicted. At 30,000 pageviews, **RPM must exceed $10 to exceed $300/month**. None of the niches has demonstrated that threshold; bookbinding is especially marginal in this scenario.

Product price, search ads and affiliate potential are commercial signals, not a formula for display-ad RPM. Do not borrow premium-network RPM claims and call them AdSense performance.

## Practical publishing direction

Choose a brandable name around screens, mesh or home repair with room to expand into insect-screen doors. Avoid a manufacturer trademark and avoid a name that implies general glazing/structural-window expertise. Domain availability and trademarks were not checked.

Suggested sections: Diagnose a Problem; Spline & Mesh; Frame Fit & Measurement; Hardware Identification; Tested Projects. Add a visual problem index. The 50-topic inventory for each leading niche is a depth check, not permission to split one answer into many thin pages.

Proposed cadence: in the first month, publish a coherent group of 8–12 genuinely useful guides from actual tests; then about two substantive articles per week if original evidence can keep up. These are planning choices, not Google requirements. Consolidate overlaps. Use the first three months to test the niche before scaling the content budget.

Include author identity, an About page, contact details, privacy information and transparent editorial/testing practices. An editorial milestone of roughly 20–30 strong articles can prompt an AdSense readiness review, but **Google does not state a universal minimum article count or guarantee approval at that count**. Its [eligibility guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en-uk) emphasizes original, quality content and policy compliance.

Google’s [AI-search documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says normal SEO requirements still apply; there is no special schema or guaranteed citation method for AI Overviews. Original evidence and useful tools are editorial advantages to test, not AI-proof traffic protection.
