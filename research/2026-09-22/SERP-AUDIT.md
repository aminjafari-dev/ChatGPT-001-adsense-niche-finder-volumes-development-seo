# Manual SERP audit — 22 September 2026

Engine: Bing, inspected through browser. These are visible organic result labels/domains in displayed order, excluding generated-answer citations, image suggestions, paid ads, and video carousels. Some pages returned fewer than ten organic results. No US region was forced in Bing; therefore this is a discovery proxy, not a US Google ranking audit. Unrecognized domains are not verified low-DR domains. Titles, snippets, and stated dates are not proof of page quality. Google AIO, page-level referring domains, DR/KD, allintitle/KGR and complete top-three content audits are unavailable. Query times are session-level, date above.
